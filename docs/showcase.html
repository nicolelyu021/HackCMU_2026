Every trip is a map of pins. Pinlog turns them into a story.

Plan a trip as pins. Live it by dropping photos and notes onto those pins. Relive it as a hand-drawn journal and a narrated little film, made only from what you actually saw and wrote.

How it works

Everything is a pin.

Planning, photos and the recap usually live in three apps. In Pinlog they hang off one map, so the diary writes itself while you travel.

Plan

Say "Kyoto, two days, temples and food." Pins drop onto the map one by one, each with the reason it was picked.

Every pin is verified on OpenStreetMap before it lands. Unverified places never appear.

Pin

Each stop carries its day, a time window, notes and a "why this pin." Lock the ones you love, replan the rest.

Route lines, day chips, and a "today" marker on the day you are there.

Drop photos

Drag a photo onto the map. The browser reads its EXIF and it lands on the right pin before anything uploads.

Within 300 m and 2 h, in the trip's time zone. No GPS? It waits in the tray, nothing is lost.

Relive

One tap writes the script, records the voice and plays a film: title, flyover between pins, your photos, captions.

The film is a pure function of the trip data. Remotion drives the map frame by frame.

The journal

A notebook, not a feed.

"Summarize my day." → three sentences, ending with the notes they came from.Journal chat, grounded only in pins, notes and captions

  • Shelf of trips drawn as a traveler's desk; each trip opens as a map room with a pencil route sketch.
  • Scrapbook per pin: photos, notes and AI captions on cream paper with lavender tape.
  • Little film: title, MapLibre flyover Cathedral → Phipps, Ken Burns over your photos, outro "12 km · 9 places · 16 photos."
  • Works on a phone as a PWA. Add to Home Screen, shoot JPEG with Location on, and photos land on pins.

Under the hood

Runs with zero keys. Better with a few.

Every provider sits behind a port with a mock, so the whole app runs offline for rehearsal. Live keys switch on Claude planning, real geocoding and a real voice. Sign-in is Auth0 Universal Login.
