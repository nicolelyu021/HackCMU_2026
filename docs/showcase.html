Beat one · plan · 0:45

Type a sentence. Get a map.

Friday, 11 pm, on the couch

Pins drop onto the map one by one while the plan is still being written, each with the reason it was picked. Every pin is checked against OpenStreetMap before it lands. The one that can't be verified never appears.

Say: "Anything it can't find on a real map never shows up. No hallucinated cafés."

    Beat three · journal · 1:45

    It only knows what you wrote.

    Saturday, 2 pm, inside the glasshouse

    Along the way we wrote three lines. That's all the journal knows. Ask it to summarize the day and it answers from those notes, and shows you which ones. No web search, no invented weather.

    Say: "It can't hallucinate the weather because it doesn't know the weather. It's our diary answering."
    • Cathedral: took the elevator to 36, the view is the whole city.
    • Phipps: $3 extra for the fern room, worth it.
    • Warhol: the silver clouds room, we stayed 20 minutes.
    Pick a question.

    Sunday, 9 pm, back home

    Pinlog writes the script from the pins and notes, records a voice, and plays a film: a title, a flyover between pins, your photos with a slow drift, and an outro that draws the whole route.

    Say: "Every line of narration cites the note it came from. The film is a pure function of the trip."

    The promise · 2:45

    Your photos, your notes, your route. In your words.

    No stock footage. No invented weather. No "vibrant city known for its bridges."

    • Places are real: a pin only appears if it exists on the map.
    • Photos land by where and when they were taken, before they upload.
    • Every line the film says points back to the note that said it.
    • Lives on your phone as a web app. Add to Home Screen and go.
