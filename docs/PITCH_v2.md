## Slide 1 · The problem (0:00–0:20)

*Slide 1 is up: the headline and the pile of polaroids.*

Think about the last trip you actually loved. When you got home, where did it go?

The photos are in your camera roll. The plan is in a notes app. The film you meant to make never happened. Photos in a camera roll are not a memory. You lived a weekend — you remember a pile of files.

## Slide 2 · How we solve it (0:20–0:45)

*Slide 2: the Other apps / Pinlog comparison.*

So we built **Pinlog**, a map-first travel diary. Everything is a pin.

Other apps keep planning, being there, and remembering in three places. In Pinlog you plan as pins on one map, your photos land on the right pin by themselves, and one tap turns those pins into a narrated little film. One page, not three apps.

Here's a trip we actually took.
